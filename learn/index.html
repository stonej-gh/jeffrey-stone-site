The on-ramp

How the nets work

The lab notebooks tell what happened when three networks were built: one that flies a ship, one that reads a screen and one that names plants. This is the course underneath them. It starts at arithmetic and ends at attention, and every chapter is a toy you operate, the way the orbit lab teaches gravity.
