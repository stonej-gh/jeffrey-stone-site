Privacy

What Rabbitbrush keeps, and what it shares only if you say so

Rabbitbrush identifies plants from a photo. The classifier runs on your phone. A photo you take or choose is processed on the phone and is not sent anywhere by the act of identifying it.

Location, if you turn it on

Settings > Location is off until you turn it on. With it on, the app ranks wild species by how often they are recorded within about 100 km of where the photo was taken: the phone's location for a new photo, the location stored in the photo for one from your library. The lookup is a table inside the app; the location is not sent anywhere to do it. A field check keeps only the 1-degree square of latitude and longitude (about 100 km on a side) the photo was taken in, never the exact place.

What the app keeps on your phone

Under each result you can make a field check: which match was right, the plant's name when the list missed it, a note. The app keeps a record of each field check with the ranked list and a 1024 pixel copy of the photo. These stay on the phone under Files > On My iPhone > Rabbitbrush > feedback and under History in the app, where you can correct or delete them, export them as a zip, or delete them all.

What sharing sends, if you turn it on

The beta asks once, when you make your first field check, whether you want to share them. If you agree, each field check and the 1024 pixel copy of its photo are sent to the developer's iCloud database as you make them. The record carries the ranked list, your answer, your note, the app version and phone model, and a random id made on your phone for this install. With Location on, it also carries the photo's 1-degree square (about 100 km), never the exact place. The shared records are used to measure the classifier and may be used to train future versions of it.

Sharing never sends your name, your Apple ID, your contacts, or your exact location. The photo copy is re-encoded by the app and carries no camera metadata, so no GPS position travels with it. Photos you do not field-check are never sent.

Your control

Turn sharing off at any time under Settings > Sharing; later field checks then stay on the phone. Turn Location off under Settings > Location; later field checks then carry no square. A correction you make on the phone rewrites the shared copy. Deleting a record on your phone, or all of them, does not take the shared copies back: what you shared stays with the developer. To have your shared records removed, send the developer the install id shown under Settings > Sharing (nothing else identifies them). Sharing needs an iCloud account on the phone; without one, nothing is sent.

The App Store privacy label

Data collected, not linked to your identity: Photos or Videos (the photo copy of a field check), Other User Content (the field check and note), Product Interaction (the ranked list and the app's reading), Coarse Location (the 1-degree square, only with Location on). Purpose: App Functionality and Analytics (improving the classifier). Not used for tracking. Nothing is collected unless sharing is on.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and removal requests go to the support page, jeffrey-stone.com/rabbitbrush/support/, or to support@jeffrey-stone.com. A removal request quotes the install id shown under Settings > Sharing; nothing else identifies the records.
