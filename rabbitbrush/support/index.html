Support & feedback

Testing Rabbitbrush? Here's how it gets better, and how to reach me

The field check under every result is the feedback that counts most. The beta links and email are below it, for everything else.

1 · Get TestFlight 2 · Join the beta →

Already have TestFlight? Go straight to step 2 · iPhone, iOS 18 or later · limited spots.

If TestFlight asks for a redemption code, there isn't one. Close it, come back here, and tap Join again.

On Android, the beta runs through Google Play. Play admits testers by a Google Group, so joining the group comes first.

1 · Join the testers group 2 · Become a tester 3 · Get it on Google Play →

Do all three on the phone, signed in to the Google account its Play Store uses: press Join group, then Become a tester, then install · Android 10 or later · about 400 MB for the model.

Step 2 can take a few minutes to open after step 1, and step 3 opens only for testers. If Play says the app is not available, go back a step.

Tell the model where it was wrong

Under every result, tap which match was right, or name the plant when the list missed it, and add a note if you have one. With sharing on, it reaches me as you make it, and the misses are what the next training round has to answer.

This does more for the model than any email could.

Send beta feedback

For anything about the app itself, with the build number and your phone model attached automatically:

  • In the app, take a screenshot → Send Beta Feedback
  • Or use Send Feedback on the app's TestFlight page
  • On Android, email a screenshot to support@jeffrey-stone.com

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, or anything else about Rabbitbrush:

support@jeffrey-stone.com

For a bug report, include the build (Settings shows it), the phone model and the OS version.

Remove what you shared

Shared field checks carry no name. The only handle is the install id under Settings > Sharing. Send it to support@jeffrey-stone.com and the records under it are deleted.

Deleting records on your phone does not take the shared copies back. The privacy page says so, and says what sharing sends.
