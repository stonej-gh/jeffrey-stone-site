Support

Remove your scores from the world board

Spacewar sends a high score only after you turn on SHARE MY SCORES, and a shared run carries no name and no account. You can take your runs off the board yourself, or ask me to do it.

From the game

Open HIGH SCORES, go to the WORLD view, and tap SHARE MY SCORES to turn sharing off. Every run this install has shared comes off the board at once. An UNDO shows for a few seconds in case you change your mind.

Clearing the high score table on your device does not touch the board. Turning sharing off is what removes your runs.

By email

If you deleted the game and reinstalled it, or moved to a new phone or tablet, the new install has a new id and cannot reach the runs the old one shared. Write to me with the initials, score and level of each run, and I will take them down.

support@jeffrey-stone.com

Telling me whether you played on a phone or a tablet helps me find the right rows.

What is removed

A shared run is your three initials, the score, the level reached, whether you played on a phone or a tablet, the game's version and the random id of the install that sent it. Removing a run deletes all of it from the board. Nothing else about you is kept there: no name, no account, no location and no contacts.

Anything else

For questions or bug reports, write to the same address. The Spacewar privacy page sets out everything the game keeps and sends.

Standing on shoulders
