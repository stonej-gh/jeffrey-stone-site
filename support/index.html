Testing an early build? Here's how to reach me

Spacewar: Orbital Duel is in beta on iPhone, iPad and Android. Rabbitbrush: Plant ID is on the App Store for iPhone and in beta on Android. How to get each is below; your notes shape where they go next.

Spacewar: Orbital Duel

iOS & Android Beta

The game opens with a hands-on tutorial; the controls guide is a quick reference.

1 · Get TestFlight 2 · Join the beta →

Already have TestFlight? Go straight to step 2 · iPhone or iPad · limited spots. Android is below.

If TestFlight asks for a redemption code, there isn't one. Close it, come back here, and tap Join again.

On Android, the beta runs through Google Play. Play admits testers by a Google Group, so joining the group comes first.

1 · Join the testers group 2 · Become a tester 3 · Get it on Google Play →

Do all three on the phone, signed in to the Google account its Play Store uses: press Join group, then Become a tester, then install · Android 10 or later.

Step 1 opens the group's page on Google Groups. The small Join group button next to the group's name is the whole step (tap it, then Join group again in the box that opens). The button is gone once you are a member, so no button means you have already joined.

Step 2 can take a few minutes to open after step 1, and step 3 opens only for testers. If Play says the app is not available, go back a step.

Rabbitbrush: Plant ID

App Store · Android Beta

A plant identifier for the western US that works with no signal: one photo, five ranked species. What it is, and how the field check teaches the model.

iPhone, iOS 18 or later · free, no ads.

Send beta feedback

The fastest way to reach me, with your build details attached automatically:

  • In a TestFlight build of Spacewar, take a screenshot → Send Beta Feedback
  • Or use Send Feedback on the app's TestFlight page
  • On Android, or from the App Store version of Rabbitbrush, email a screenshot to support@jeffrey-stone.com
  • In Rabbitbrush, the field check under every result is the feedback that counts most

Tell me what confused you, what felt good, and anything that broke.
